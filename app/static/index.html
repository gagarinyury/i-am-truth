I Am Truth

Methodology audit for biomedical papers — and an honest account of how much of it could be grounded in the data.

A 2026 study made headlines as “GLP-1 drugs cut breast cancer by 35%.” In the paper itself the absolute difference is 0.69 percentage points, NNT 144 — and the treated group started out sicker (Charlson 5+: 19.7% vs 10.4%) yet had fewer cancers. Both of those numbers live in an appendix table on page ten. This tool goes and gets them.
